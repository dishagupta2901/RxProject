If a repo's 10 open issues ran through full automation

No real repository here — this maps the outcome you'd typically expect if an autonomous agent triaged, fixed, and verified ten arbitrary issues end‑to‑end with zero human intervention. Ratios are assumptions based on common fix-difficulty distributions, not a measurement.

Assumption ~60% of issues are self-contained (single-file, reproducible, clear acceptance criteria) — these clear automation end-to-end.
Assumption ~30% touch ambiguous specs or cross-module effects — a patch lands but confidence is low enough to route to a human.
Assumption ~10% have no test coverage to verify against, or genuinely need a product decision — full automation stalls without one.
Caveat Real ratios shift hard with codebase test coverage, issue quality, and how much repo-specific context the agent is given.

This is an illustrative model, not a forecast for any specific repository — share the actual 10 issues and I can score each one individually instead of assuming a distribution.
